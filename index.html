Logins

Disabling a login blocks it everywhere immediately. If someone forgets their password, disable their old login and create a new one for them — this app can't force-set a password on an existing login without a paid backend.

Label print alignment

If printed labels are landing shifted left/right or up/down on the die-cut stickers (the printer's own registration, not something the app can know on its own), nudge them here and print a test pair to check. This is saved on this device only — a different phone/computer printing to a different printer may need its own nudge.

The test pair prints with a dashed outline right at the edge of each 50×25mm box, so you can see exactly how far off the die-cut edge it lands and in which direction. Adjust in small steps (0.5–1mm) and reprint until the dashed line sits just inside the sticker's edge on all sides.

Danger zone

Permanently erases every product, stock figure, generated carton/packet barcode, and movement-history entry — the app goes back to completely empty, as if freshly set up. Logins are not affected. This cannot be undone.
